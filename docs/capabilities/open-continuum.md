# Open the Continuum

A writer can keep the **Continuum** beside other notes or place it elsewhere in the Obsidian workspace.

## Behaviors
- The Continuum view uses the infinity icon.
- The Continuum initially opens in the right sidebar.
- Obsidian restores the pane only when it was previously open; closing it is respected across restarts.
- Adding content or invoking “Focus Continuum” opens a currently closed pane.
- The pane can be dragged into the main area, another sidebar, or another supported Obsidian workspace location.
- Obsidian preserves the user’s chosen placement while the pane remains open.
- After closure, reopening uses the last broad area—left sidebar, right sidebar, or main area—and falls back to the right sidebar when unavailable.
- A “Focus Continuum” command reveals a collapsed pane and restores keyboard focus to its last-focused entry.
- No global hotkey is assigned by default; users can assign one in Obsidian’s Hotkeys settings.

## Out of scope
- A ribbon icon or status-bar control.
- Permanently forcing the Continuum into a specific workspace location.
- Obsidian Mobile support; Continuum is desktop-only.
